The Complete Guide to AI Trading Bots, MEV, Flashloans, and Arbitrage Agents

What Are AI Trading Bots?

AI trading bots are autonomous programs that use machine learning, statistical arbitrage, and real-time data feeds to execute trades without human intervention. In DeFi, these bots monitor the mempool, DEX liquidity, and lending protocols to find profitable opportunities. Modern AI trading bots can adapt to changing market conditions, learn from historical data, and optimise execution for maximum profit and minimal gas cost.

Understanding MEV Sandwich Attacks

MEV sandwich (also spelled sanddwich) refers to a strategy where a bot spots a large pending transaction in the mempool, then places two orders around it — one before and one after — to profit from the victim's price slippage. The bot essentially "sandwiches" the victim's trade. Successful MEV sandwich bots rely on low-latency infrastructure, optimised gas bidding, and precise bundle construction.

Flashloan Arbitrage Explained

Flashloan arbitrage is one of the lowest-risk DeFi strategies. A flashloan is an uncollateralized loan that must be borrowed and repaid within a single atomic transaction. The bot borrows a large amount, buys an asset on one DEX where it's cheap, sells it on another where it's expensive, repays the flashloan plus fees, and keeps the difference. If the trade isn't profitable at execution time, the whole transaction reverts — meaning you never actually take on debt.

Why Flashloan Bots Are Popular

  • No upfront capital required — loans are uncollateralized.
  • Atomic execution — unprofitable trades simply revert.
  • Works across dozens of DEXs and chains simultaneously.
  • Can be combined with MEV strategies for compounded returns.

Liquidation Arbitrage Bots

Liquidation arbitrage (or simply "liq botting") involves monitoring lending protocols such as Aave, Compound, Venus, and Euler. When a borrower's collateral ratio falls below the liquidation threshold, liquidators can repay a portion of the debt and receive the collateral at a discount (typically 5–15%). AI liquidation bots compete on gas price and precision to win these auctions. This strategy is considered lower-risk because the profit is fixed at the moment of execution.

Autonomous Arbitrage Agents

Arbitrage agents are the next evolution of DeFi bots. Unlike simple scripts, these are autonomous agents that scan multiple chains, discover complex arbitrage paths (including triangular and cross-chain routes), and execute them without human instruction. They use reinforcement learning to improve over time, and they can cooperate or compete with other agents in the ecosystem.

While DeFi arbitrage strategies like flashloan arbitrage and liquidation bots are considered lower-risk, they are not risk-free. Always test strategies in a simulated environment first, never invest more than you can afford to lose, and understand the smart contract risks involved in DeFi.
